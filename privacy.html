Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 10, 2026

1. Data we access

When you authorize the App with TikTok, we receive an access token and your TikTok Open ID. We use them only to publish the videos you choose to your own account. If you use Twitch features, we access only public Twitch data and the clips you select.

2. How we use data

Data is used solely to provide the posting function. We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Storage

Access tokens are stored locally on the device running the App. Video files are processed locally and are not retained after upload unless you keep them yourself.

4. Revoking access

You can revoke the App's access at any time in TikTok under Settings → Security → Manage app permissions. After revocation, the stored token becomes invalid and can be deleted.

5. Your rights

You may request deletion of any data related to you by contacting us.
